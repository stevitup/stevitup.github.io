The Sweet Beaver

E-commerce · Live on Render

The Sweet Beaver

Built with ChatGPT. My role: product owner, requirements, testing and deployment.

A full-stack online bakery shop with a product catalog, cart, checkout and order tracking. Customers can shop in English or Spanish, and the owner runs everything from a custom-branded admin.

  • Stripe card and wallet payments alongside manual methods, each switchable on or off
  • Manual payments stay “Awaiting Payment” until verified, so an order is never marked paid on a customer's word alone
  • Pickup-slot scheduling that reveals the pickup address only at the right checkout step
  • Private order confirmation links, customer profiles and marketing-subscriber export
  • Deployed with persistent media storage, secrets in environment variables, sitemap and robots.txt for SEO

Alimac Mysteries

Alimac Mysteries

Built with Claude. My role: product owner, requirements, testing and deployment.

An online shop and auction platform for a vintage, antique and decor business, with the rules real auctions need: proxy bidding, optional reserve prices and scheduled endings, plus a regular storefront and a sales dashboard for the owner.

  • Proxy bidding, reserve prices and scheduled auction endings
  • A custom background job that closes auctions, enforces a 48-hour payment window and sends second-chance offers to the next-highest bidder
  • Email-verified accounts with saved payment cards
  • Local pickup or shipping to the US, Canada and Mexico, with automatic sales tax
  • Automated tests; developed across macOS and Windows with Git

About me

A non-traditional path, on purpose.

I'm Steven, a Google-certified data analyst based in Lawrenceville, Georgia, who builds web apps with AI. I studied at Georgia State University, and my creative background began with acting, dancing, and singing at age 15 before expanding into directing, cinematography, video editing, and photography during college. Since 2018, I've combined those creative skills with technology through freelance work for clients, bringing a visual and storytelling perspective to the way I approach data, design, and web development.

For the past five years I've worked in tech-facing customer roles with Verizon, helping people make sense of complex plans and devices and training new teammates to do the same. Along the way I started building websites with AI: The Sweet Beaver with ChatGPT, then Alimac Mysteries and this portfolio with Claude. I own the ideas, the requirements and the testing, the AI writes most of the code, and I keep learning how it all works.

Now I'm moving into tech full time. My goal is to grow into a technical leader who can direct the build, read the data, lead the team, and keep the people using the product at the center of every decision.

Off the clock, you'll find me gaming, watching soccer or planning my next trip. Fun fact: I can tap dance.
